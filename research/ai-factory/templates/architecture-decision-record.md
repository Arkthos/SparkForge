## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, existing capabilities, relevant data boundaries, and why a decision is needed now.

## Decision drivers

Prioritize the actual requirements: correctness, operational ownership, privacy, latency, cost, recovery, portability, or another explicitly stated need.

Do not use a vendor's popularity as a substitute for a requirement.

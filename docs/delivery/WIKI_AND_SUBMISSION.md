# Documentation and contribution delivery

WP-170 through WP-173 prepare the first qualified local release; WP-174 performs actual platform submission only through its selected scope/route. A development-repository push does not establish organizational acceptance or deployment.

## Local bundle

Include exact source/package inventory, supported environment/host/profile versions, current behavioral and review/controller evidence, user/operator/contributor guidance, setup/recovery/lifecycle instructions, retained limitations, genuine owner/support/terms decisions and the requested review stage. Exclude credentials, private snapshots, real journals and raw model transcripts.

## Contribution preview

Prepare a complete local diff/PR/wiki payload preview against the actual destination contract when available. Bind exact target/payload/evidence and show changes before effects. New or changed destination/payload/authority outside the envelope requires its decision owner. Unknown external write outcomes reconcile before replay.

Do not invent platform-specific wiki templates, contribution commands, licensing or reviewers. Source-neutral preparation can proceed until those details are genuinely needed. Historical GitHub publication and private-corpus review material is in the [archive](../archive/README.md); it is not a current development prerequisite.

## Exit

A reviewer can understand the delivered behavior, what was actually tested, remaining risks and exact requested action. Installation, submission, platform acceptance and production deployment remain separately observed outcomes.
